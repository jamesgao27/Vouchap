# Vouchap 2.1.5

## What's New

- **Permissions**: Updated privacy permission descriptions for camera, photo library, and microphone to meet App Store Review Guidelines (5.1.1). Descriptions now clearly state that Vouchap uses these for scanning and uploading receipts for digital tracking.
- **Email links**: Fixed errors when opening expired or invalid email links.

## Bug Fixes

- Resolved App Store rejection related to vague camera usage description.
- Improved handling of deep links and email-based app opens.
