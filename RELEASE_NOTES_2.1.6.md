# Vouchap 2.1.6

## What's New

- **Receipt scanning**: Fixed errors when scanning receipts when a supplier or customer name already exists. The app now uses the existing record instead of failing.
- **Permissions**: Updated camera, photo library, and microphone permission descriptions to meet App Store guidelines and clarify how Vouchap uses them for receipt scanning and digital tracking.

## Bug Fixes

- Photo recognition no longer shows "supplier/customer name already exists" as an error during scan; duplicate names are handled automatically.
- Improved handling of duplicate names for suppliers, customers, accounts, warehouses, locations, and SKUs during recognition and manual editing.
